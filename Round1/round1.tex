\underline{\textbf{Instructions}}:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item This round consists of \textbf{3 sections} with \textbf{100 questions in total}.
  %\textbf{row}, \textbf{column}, and \textbf{\(3\times 3\) blocks}.
  \item Write your answers in the blank column to the right of their respective questions. 
  \item You may use the blank spaces provided on the paper for rough work.
  \item You may not use a calculator or anything that is able to assist you in calculations.
  \item For numbers 4 digits and above, you may separate each triple digit block with a \textbf{space} or \textbf{comma}. For example: "four thousand, four hundred and forty four" may be written as "4444", "4,444" or "4 444".
  \item The time limit is \textbf{30 minutes}.
  \item \textbf{ONE} point will be awarded for each correct answer. \textbf{ZERO} points will be awarded for each wrong answer.    
  \item {\bf No} points will be deducted for incorrect answers.
  \item Do not round any of your answers. For example, if your answer was 44.444, do not round it to 44.44, 44.4 etc. The answers may be \textbf{negative}.
  \item Bonus points of up to 25 marks will be awarded to participants who complete all questions with 100\% accuracy before the time limit written in point 6 elapses. 
  \item Once the time limit is up, no participant should write anything on his/her paper. Anyone caught doing so will be given a 20-point penalty. 
  \item Do not turn over the cover page until you are told to do so.
  \item Penalty {\bf will} be imposed on illegible handwriting - we cannot mark what we cannot read. 
\end{enumerate}
